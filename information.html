Fr. Conceicao Rodrigues College of Engineering (FRCRCE), located in Bandra, Mumbai, India, is a prestigious institution founded in 1984 under the patronage of the Agnel Ashram Fathers. Affiliated with the University of Mumbai and approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), FRCRCE offers a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs in engineering disciplines such as BTech, MTech, and PhD. The college boasts a modern campus equipped with state-of-the-art facilities, including laboratories, libraries, and sports amenities, fostering a conducive environment for learning and growth. Committed to academic excellence, FRCRCE has a dedicated faculty known for their expertise and mentorship, guiding students toward success in their chosen fields. The college's strong placement cell ensures promising career prospects for graduates through internships and job placements with leading companies. Beyond academics, FRCRCE promotes a vibrant campus life, encouraging students to engage in extracurricular activities such as sports, cultural events, and social initiatives, shaping well-rounded individuals ready to make a positive impact in the world. Furthermore, students also get on-campus placement evey year where the highest package goes up to INR 17 LPA whereas the average package is recorded at INR 8.2 LPA. The most prominent recruiters of FRCRCE are JP Morgan, Siemens, Reliance, Amazon, Accenture, and many more.
